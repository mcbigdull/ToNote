Plain MIDI removes pitch bends. Thus sustained vibrato becomes a fixed semitone
center, and false repeated note-ons become repeated same-pitch score notes.

extra voices do not mean that the whole violin is silent. Original MIDI export
does not encode the 3/4 meter shown in the screenshot; it uses fixed 120 BPM as a
time container. Screenshot tempo 147 and bar divisions are not model estimates.

miss harmonics that span several fragmented primary notes. Its previous count
